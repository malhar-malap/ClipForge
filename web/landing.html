FAQ

Good questions.

What is an AI clipping tool?+

It is software that watches a long video for you and cuts it into short clips automatically. ClipForge uses AI to find the moments with a strong hook and payoff, frames them 9:16, adds captions, and writes a title and hashtags, ready for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and TikTok.

What is a free preview?+

Sign up, paste a video, and ClipForge plays its best 3 clips in your browser with captions and a watermark. No card needed.

What counts as a clip?+

Each clip made in full quality counts as one from your plan, whether it is 20 seconds or a minute. Clips that fail are not counted, and finding moments and previewing are free.

Does it work for gaming streams?+

Yes. Your facecam is found and put on top, and HUD elements like health bars and minimaps stay visible. Console recordings work too.

Which languages are supported?+

English, Hindi (with Hinglish captions), Urdu, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Spanish, Portuguese, Arabic and many more.

What is BYOK?+

"Bring your own key": on the BYOK plans you connect your own AI key from Claude, OpenAI, Gemini and others, so the plan costs less. The AI work for your clips runs on your key and your AI provider bills you for it directly, on top of the ClipForge price. We test the key, store it encrypted and never show it again.
